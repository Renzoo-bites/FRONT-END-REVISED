About FindIt

SWAFO's digital lost and found for the campus community.

FindIt replaces the paper logbook at the SWAFO office. Students and employees can browse items that have been turned in, report something they lost or found, and claim their belongings online before picking them up in person.

How it works

  1. Report or browse

    Post a lost or found item, or look through what's been turned in.

  2. Claim with proof

    Describe details only the owner would know so SWAFO can verify the claim.

  3. Pick it up

    Once approved, bring a valid ID to the SWAFO office to get your item back.
